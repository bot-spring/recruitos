# **Chapter 1: A Client Needs to Hire**

## **Every Placement Begins with a Business Need**

Every successful placement starts long before a recruiter searches for candidates. It begins when a company realizes it needs to hire.

The reason may vary. A fast-growing startup may be expanding its engineering team. An enterprise may need to replace an employee who has resigned. A manufacturing company may be opening a new plant. Regardless of the reason, the business challenge is the same—they need the right person, and they need them quickly.

For most recruitment agencies, this stage is often managed through emails, phone calls, spreadsheets, and informal conversations. Important details get lost, follow-ups are delayed, ownership becomes unclear, and every recruiter works differently.

RecruitOS transforms this fragmented process into a structured and repeatable workflow.

Instead of simply collecting a hiring request, RecruitOS converts every enquiry into a controlled business process that ensures accountability, visibility, and operational consistency from the very beginning.

---

## **The People Involved**

Although the process appears simple, several stakeholders participate in this first stage of the recruitment lifecycle.

### **The Client**

The client is an organization looking to fill one or more positions. They provide the hiring requirements, business expectations, budget, timelines, and commercial agreement.

Their expectation is simple:

> "I want the right candidates as quickly as possible without repeatedly following up with the recruitment agency."

---

### **The Agency Owner or Team Lead**

The Agency Owner or Team Lead is responsible for deciding whether the agency should accept the hiring assignment.

Before committing resources, they evaluate questions such as:

* Is this a genuine hiring opportunity?  
* Are the commercial terms acceptable?  
* Does the agency have the capability to deliver?  
* Should this assignment be prioritized over others?

Only after these questions are answered does the hiring request become an official mandate.

---

### **The Recruiter**

At this stage, the recruiter is not yet involved in sourcing candidates.

Their involvement begins only after the hiring mandate has been approved and assigned to them.

Their responsibility is to execute the hiring assignment successfully.

---

## **The Journey Begins**

Every hiring mandate begins with a business opportunity, but that opportunity can arrive through multiple channels.

Some clients discover the agency through its website and submit a **hiring request online-1**. Others may reach out after speaking with the **Business Development team(Offline-2),** through referrals, networking events, existing client relationships, email enquiries, phone calls, or even WhatsApp conversations.

Regardless of how the opportunity is generated, RecruitOS ensures that every hiring requirement enters the organization through a single, standardized intake process.

The hiring requirement typically captures information such as:

* Company details  
* Position to be hired  
* Number of openings  
* Required experience  
* Location  
* Compensation range  
* Hiring priority  
* Commercial engagement model

If the requirement is received offline by the Business Development team, they record the same information within RecruitOS on behalf of the client. If the client submits the requirement online, the information is captured automatically.

From this point onward, there is no distinction between an online or offline enquiry. Every hiring requirement follows the same approval, onboarding, and assignment process, ensuring that nothing falls through the cracks and every opportunity is managed consistently.

At this stage, the requirement has **not** yet become an active recruitment assignment.

It is still an opportunity that must be reviewed before agency resources are committed.

---

## **Evaluating the Opportunity**

The new enquiry immediately appears in the operational dashboard of the Agency Owner or Team Lead.

Before accepting the assignment, they review the complete hiring request.

Typical decisions include:

* Should this client be onboarded?  
* Does the hiring requirement contain enough information?  
* Are commercial terms acceptable?  
* Is additional clarification required before proceeding?  
* Which recruiter would be best suited for this assignment?

The objective is not simply to approve every enquiry, but to ensure that the agency invests its time in opportunities that align with its expertise and business goals.

---

## **What RecruitOS Does Automatically**

While the Agency Owner focuses on business decisions, RecruitOS quietly performs several operational tasks in the background.

It validates whether the client already exists in the agency's database, helping prevent duplicate client records.

Once the hiring request is approved, RecruitOS automatically creates the client profile (if required), converts the enquiry into an active hiring mandate, records the agreed commercial terms, and sends an onboarding confirmation to the client's primary contact.

The platform also establishes complete ownership of the assignment by recording who approved it, when it was approved, and which recruiter will be responsible for delivering the mandate.

From this point onward, every activity related to the assignment can be tracked and measured.

---

## **Assigning Ownership**

Once the hiring mandate becomes active, the Agency Owner assigns it to a recruiter.

This is more than a simple allocation of work.

Ownership now shifts from business acquisition to recruitment execution.

The assigned recruiter becomes responsible for:

* Understanding the hiring requirement.  
* Building the sourcing strategy.  
* Presenting suitable candidates.  
* Managing communication with the client.  
* Driving the assignment through to successful placement.

RecruitOS starts monitoring the assignment from the moment it is allocated, ensuring that service levels, follow-ups, and recruiter activity remain visible throughout the lifecycle.

---

## **End of Chapter**

The business opportunity has now become an active recruitment mandate.

The client has been successfully onboarded.

Commercial terms have been agreed.

Ownership has been assigned.

The recruiter opens their dashboard and sees a new active hiring assignment waiting to be fulfilled.

The real recruitment journey is about to begin.

The next chapter explores how RecruitOS helps recruiters identify, source, and organize the right candidates before the search even begins.

# **Chapter 2: Finding the Right Candidate**

## **Every Successful Placement Begins with the Right Search Strategy**

Receiving a hiring mandate is only the beginning of the recruitment journey.

The real challenge now begins.

How does a recruiter identify the right candidate among thousands of professionals, multiple sourcing channels, and an ever-changing talent market?

Traditional recruitment often relies heavily on memory, spreadsheets, job portals, and individual recruiter experience. One recruiter may remember speaking to a candidate six months ago, while another starts the search from scratch. Duplicate resumes get submitted, strong candidates are overlooked, and valuable recruiter time is spent performing repetitive work.

RecruitOS transforms candidate sourcing from an individual effort into an organized business process.

Instead of asking,

> "Where should I search?"

RecruitOS first asks,

> "Do we already know the right candidate?"

---

## **The People Involved**

At this stage, the recruitment process expands beyond the client and the agency.

Several participants now contribute to building the candidate pipeline.

### **The Recruiter**

The recruiter is responsible for building the strongest possible shortlist for the client.

Their objective is not simply to collect resumes, but to identify candidates who genuinely match the client's hiring requirements while minimizing turnaround time.

---

### **Candidates**

Candidates may discover opportunities through multiple channels.

Some apply directly through the agency's career portal.

Others arrive through job boards, referrals, social media, networking, recruiters, or previous interactions with the agency.

Regardless of where they come from, every candidate follows the same structured evaluation process.

---

### **Partner Recruiters**

During periods of high demand or niche hiring, the agency may collaborate with trusted recruitment partners.

Partners contribute additional candidate pipelines while RecruitOS ensures ownership, commercial agreements, and candidate rights remain protected.

---

## **The Search Begins**

The recruiter opens the newly assigned hiring mandate.

Before searching external platforms, RecruitOS performs the first and most important search—its own database.

The system checks whether suitable candidates already exist based on factors such as skills, experience, location, industry background, notice period, salary expectation  and previous interactions.

Often, the agency has already invested significant time building relationships with qualified candidates.

RecruitOS ensures that this knowledge is never lost.

If suitable candidates are found, the recruiter can immediately begin engaging them.

If not, the search expands to external sourcing channels.

---

## **Building the Talent Pipeline**

Recruiters can source candidates from multiple channels, including:

* The agency's career portal  
* Job portals  
* Professional networking platforms  
* Employee and candidate referrals  
* Business networks  
* Recruitment partners  
* Manual recruiter outreach

Every candidate, regardless of source, enters RecruitOS through a single standardized process.

This ensures that the agency maintains one trusted talent database rather than disconnected collections of resumes.

---

## **What RecruitOS Does Automatically**

While recruiters focus on identifying great talent, RecruitOS quietly performs several operational tasks in the background.

Every incoming resume is automatically analyzed and converted into structured candidate information.

The platform identifies key details such as experience, skills, current employer, notice period, compensation expectations, and contact information.

Before creating a new candidate profile, RecruitOS checks whether the individual already exists in the agency's database.

This prevents duplicate records, duplicate submissions, and ownership conflicts between recruiters or recruitment partners.

When candidates arrive through partner recruiters, RecruitOS automatically validates ownership rules, ensuring that commercial agreements and candidate rights are respected before the profile enters the active talent pool.

The platform also preserves relationships between candidates whenever they are relevant—for example, spouses relocating together or candidates referred by one another—allowing recruiters to identify opportunities that might otherwise be missed.

---

## **Leveraging Existing Agency Knowledge**

One of the greatest assets of any recruitment agency is not its access to job portals.

It is the knowledge accumulated from every previous hiring assignment.

Candidates who narrowly missed selection for one role may be ideal for another.

RecruitOS continuously searches this historical talent pool and recommends previously evaluated candidates whenever a new hiring mandate resembles earlier assignments.

Instead of rebuilding the search from the beginning, recruiters can immediately reconnect with proven talent, reducing sourcing time and improving placement speed.

---

## **The Recruiter's Decision**

At this stage, the recruiter has assembled a pool of qualified candidates.

Some are existing relationships.

Some are newly discovered.

Others may have been introduced by trusted recruitment partners.

The recruiter's responsibility is now to evaluate these candidates, begin conversations, validate interest, and prepare a shortlist that best aligns with the client's expectations.

RecruitOS provides the intelligence, organization, and visibility required to support this decision, but the final judgment always remains with the recruiter.

---

## **End of Chapter**

The recruiter now has a structured and qualified talent pipeline.

Every candidate has been organized, validated, and prepared for evaluation.

The next step is no longer searching for talent.

It is presenting the right talent to the client.

The next chapter explores how RecruitOS enables recruiters to collaborate with clients, gather structured feedback, and move candidates confidently into the interview process.

# **Chapter 3: Presenting the Right Candidates to the Client**

## **Business Situation**

The recruiter has completed the sourcing process and built a pool of qualified candidates.

After evaluating resumes, speaking with candidates, and validating their interest, the recruiter is now ready to present the best candidates to the client.

The objective is not to send every available profile. It is to present a carefully shortlisted set of candidates that closely match the client's hiring requirements.

---

## **Who is Involved**

### **Recruiter**

Owns the candidate shortlist, communicates with the client, and manages the hiring process.

### **Client Hiring Team**

Reviews the shortlisted candidates, provides feedback, and decides which candidates should move to the interview stage.

### **Candidate**

Waits for the client's decision while remaining engaged through the recruitment process.

---

## **Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Recruiter Finalizes the Shortlist**

The recruiter reviews all qualified candidates and selects those who best meet the client's requirements.

Each shortlisted candidate includes:

* Resume  
* Experience summary  
* Skills  
* Current compensation  
* Expected compensation  
* Notice period  
* Recruiter notes

---

### **Step 2 — Shortlist is Shared with the Client**

The recruiter sends the shortlisted candidates to the client through RecruitOS.

The client receives a secure review link that works without requiring them to log in.

This allows the client to review candidates from any device with minimal effort.

---

### **Step 3 — Client Reviews Each Candidate**

The client reviews every shortlisted profile individually.

For each candidate, the client can:

* Shortlist for Interview  
* Keep on Hold  
* Reject

If the client rejects a candidate, they must provide the reason for rejection.

This helps the recruiter better understand the client's expectations for future submissions.

---

### **Step 4 — Recruiter Reviews Client Feedback**

As client decisions are received, RecruitOS updates the recruitment pipeline.

The recruiter immediately knows:

* Which candidates should move forward.  
* Which candidates should remain on hold.  
* Which candidates need replacement.

This allows the recruiter to continue working without waiting for manual follow-ups.

---

### **Step 5 — Client Follow-up**

If the client has not reviewed the shortlisted candidates within the expected timeframe, RecruitOS automatically sends polite reminders.

The recruiter remains informed throughout the process without having to manually chase every pending review.

---

## **Key Business Decisions**

During this stage, several important decisions are made.

The client decides:

* Which candidates deserve an interview.  
* Which candidates require additional evaluation.  
* Which candidates are not suitable.

The recruiter decides:

* Whether additional candidates should be sourced.  
* Whether feedback indicates a change in search strategy.  
* Whether the hiring mandate needs refinement.

---

## **What RecruitOS Does Automatically**

Throughout this workflow, RecruitOS continuously supports both the recruiter and the client.

It automatically:

* Creates secure candidate review links.  
* Protects candidate personal information.  
* Records every client decision.  
* Captures rejection reasons.  
* Updates candidate pipeline stages.  
* Sends client reminder notifications when reviews are pending.  
* Maintains a complete audit trail of every action and decision.

---

## **End of Chapter**

The client has now completed the first evaluation of the shortlisted candidates.

Suitable candidates have been selected for interviews, while unsuccessful candidates have been recorded with structured feedback.

The recruitment process now moves into interview coordination, where RecruitOS helps both the client and candidate schedule interviews with minimal manual effort.

# **Chapter 4: Managing Interviews**

## **Business Situation**

The client has reviewed the shortlisted candidates and selected those they wish to interview.

The objective of this stage is to efficiently coordinate interviews between the client and the candidate while minimizing manual communication, scheduling conflicts, and delays.

---

## **Who is Involved**

### **Recruiter**

Coordinates the interview process, ensures both parties are aligned, and manages any scheduling conflicts.

### **Client Hiring Team**

Provides interview availability, conducts the interviews, and shares interview outcomes.

### **Candidate**

Confirms interview availability, attends the interview, and provides post-interview feedback.

---

## **Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Client Selects Candidates for Interview**

After reviewing the shortlisted candidates, the client marks the candidates they wish to interview.

RecruitOS immediately notifies the assigned recruiter that interview scheduling can begin.

---

### **Step 2 — Client Shares Interview Availability**

The client provides multiple interview time slots based on the interview panel's availability.

These slots are shared with the candidate through RecruitOS.

---

### **Step 3 — Candidate Confirms Availability**

The candidate reviews the proposed interview slots.

If one of the proposed slots is suitable, the candidate confirms the preferred time.

The interview is automatically scheduled and both parties receive confirmation.

If none of the proposed slots are suitable, the candidate requests alternative timings.

RecruitOS creates a follow-up task for the recruiter to coordinate a mutually convenient schedule with the client.

---

### **Step 4 — Interview Preparation**

Twenty-four hours before the scheduled interview, RecruitOS automatically shares an interview preparation package with the candidate.

The preparation package may include:

* Company overview  
* Job summary  
* Interview format  
* Interviewer's details (where applicable)  
* Preparation guidelines

The objective is to help candidates attend interviews with confidence and improve overall interview quality.

---

### **Step 5 — Interview Takes Place**

The interview is conducted as scheduled.

If the interview proceeds successfully, the recruiter waits for feedback from both the client and the candidate.

If either party reports a no-show, RecruitOS immediately flags the interview for attention and creates a high-priority task for the recruiter to investigate and coordinate the next steps.

---

### **Step 6 — Post-Interview Feedback**

Shortly after the interview, RecruitOS requests feedback from the candidate.

The recruiter also follows up with the client to capture their interview outcome.

Before the recruitment process can move forward, an interview outcome must be recorded.

Possible outcomes include:

* Selected for Next Round  
* Selected for Offer  
* Rejected  
* Reschedule Required  
* Interview Not Conducted

This ensures that every interview concludes with a clear business decision before progressing further.

---

## **Key Business Decisions**

During this stage, the following decisions are made:

The client decides:

* Whether the candidate progresses.  
* Whether another interview round is required.  
* Whether the candidate should be rejected.

The candidate decides:

* Whether they remain interested in the opportunity.  
* Whether they can attend the proposed schedule.

The recruiter decides:

* Whether additional coordination is required.  
* Whether replacement candidates should begin entering the pipeline.

---

## **What RecruitOS Does Automatically**

Throughout the interview process, RecruitOS automatically:

* Notifies recruiters when candidates are shortlisted for interviews.  
* Shares interview slots with candidates.  
* Confirms interview schedules.  
* Creates coordination tasks when alternative timings are requested.  
* Sends interview preparation material before the interview.  
* Collects post-interview candidate feedback.  
* Detects interview no-shows and raises alerts.  
* Ensures an interview outcome is recorded before the candidate can progress to the next stage.

---

## **End of Chapter**

The interview process is now complete.

The client has evaluated the candidate, the candidate has shared their feedback, and the interview outcome has been recorded.

If the candidate is successful, the recruitment journey moves into offer management.

# **Chapter 5: Securing the Offer**

## **Business Situation**

The client has successfully completed the interview process and identified the preferred candidate.

The objective of this stage is to convert the selected candidate into a confirmed employee while minimizing the risk of offer declines, counter offers, or candidate drop-offs before joining.

This is one of the most critical stages of the recruitment lifecycle, as every successful offer directly impacts the agency's revenue.

---

## **Who is Involved**

### **Recruiter**

Acts as the primary coordinator between the client and the candidate, ensuring the offer is accepted and the candidate remains engaged until joining.

### **Client Hiring Team**

Prepares and releases the official employment offer and confirms the final compensation package.

### **Candidate**

Reviews the offer, accepts or declines it, submits required documents, and completes the notice period before joining.

### **Agency Owner / Team Lead**

Reviews and approves commercial changes where required and monitors high-risk placements.

---

## **Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Client Selects the Final Candidate**

Once all interview rounds are complete, the client selects the successful candidate.

The recruiter is notified to begin the offer process.

---

### **Step 2 — Offer Details are Finalized**

The recruiter records the final offer details received from the client.

These include:

* Fixed Compensation  
* Variable Compensation  
* Joining Date  
* Employment Location  
* Other commercial terms

RecruitOS calculates the agency's placement fee based on the agreed commercial agreement with the client.

---

### **Step 3 — Candidate Receives and Accepts the Offer**

The candidate reviews the employment offer and confirms their decision.

If the candidate accepts, the recruitment process moves into the notice period.

If the candidate declines, the recruiter is notified immediately so that alternative candidates can be considered.

---

### **Step 4 — Notice Period Monitoring Begins**

Once the offer is accepted, RecruitOS starts monitoring the candidate throughout the notice period.

At regular intervals, the candidate receives simple check-ins to understand:

* Whether resignation has been accepted.  
* Whether the notice period is progressing as expected.  
* Whether they have received a counter offer.  
* Whether they still intend to join.

These regular interactions help the recruiter identify risks long before the joining date.

---

### **Step 5 — Managing Candidate Risk**

If the candidate responds positively, the recruitment process continues as planned.

If the candidate reports concerns such as counter offers, delays, or uncertainty, RecruitOS immediately highlights the placement as high risk so the recruiter can intervene.

If the candidate repeatedly fails to respond, the risk level increases and the matter is escalated to the Agency Owner or Team Lead for additional support.

---

### **Step 6 — Compliance Document Collection**

During the notice period, the candidate is requested to submit all required joining documents.

These may include:

* Identity Proof  
* Educational Certificates  
* Salary Documents includes appointment letters, salary slips  
* Relieving Letter  
* Any other documents requested by the client

RecruitOS tracks document submission and helps ensure everything is completed before the joining date.

---

### **Step 7 — Preparing for Joining**

As the joining date approaches, RecruitOS verifies that:

* The offer has been accepted.  
* Required documents have been received.  
* The candidate is still committed to joining.  
* The client is ready for onboarding.

Once everything is complete, the candidate is ready to join the organization.

---

## **Key Business Decisions**

During this stage, several important business decisions are made.

The candidate decides:

* Whether to accept the offer.  
* Whether to continue despite counter offers.  
* Whether to complete the notice period.

The client decides:

* Whether the final offer reflects the agreed terms.  
* Whether all onboarding requirements have been completed.

The recruiter decides:

* Whether additional engagement is required.  
* Whether the placement is at risk.  
* Whether management intervention is required.

---

## **What RecruitOS Does Automatically**

Throughout this workflow, RecruitOS continuously supports the recruitment team by:

* Recording final offer details.  
* Calculating placement fees.  
* Tracking offer acceptance.  
* Scheduling periodic notice period check-ins.  
* Identifying candidates at risk of dropping out.  
* Escalating high-risk cases to management.  
* Tracking compliance document submission.  
* Preparing both the agency and the client for the candidate's joining.

---

## **End of Chapter**

The candidate has successfully accepted the offer, completed the notice period, and is fully prepared to join the client organization.

The focus now shifts from securing the placement to successfully completing it.

# **Chapter 6: Completing the Placement & Protecting Revenue**

## **Business Situation**

The candidate has accepted the offer and successfully completed the notice period.

The agency's objective is now to ensure the candidate joins the client organization, complete all commercial activities, and protect the placement during the probation period.

Although the hardest part of recruitment appears to be over, this is one of the most critical stages for the agency. A candidate withdrawing at the last moment or leaving during probation can directly impact revenue, client relationships, and future business.

RecruitOS helps agencies monitor every placement until it is successfully completed.

---

## **Who is Involved**

### **Recruiter**

Ensures the candidate joins successfully, maintains communication with both the client and the candidate, and responds quickly if any risks arise.

### **Client HR**

Confirms the candidate's joining and completes the onboarding process.

### **Candidate**

Joins the organization and begins employment.

### **Agency Owner / Team Lead**

Monitors high-value placements, approves financial actions, and oversees replacement cases when required.

### **Finance Team**

Tracks invoices, client payments, and partner settlements.

---

## **Workflow**

### **Step 1 — Candidate Successfully Joins**

On the scheduled joining date, the client confirms that the candidate has officially joined the organization.

RecruitOS records the joining date and marks the placement as successfully completed.

---

### **Step 2 — Invoice is Generated**

Once the joining is confirmed, RecruitOS prepares the placement invoice based on the commercial agreement with the client.

The invoice is shared with the client for payment processing.

The agency can now begin tracking its expected revenue from the placement.

---

### **Step 3 — Probation Monitoring Begins**

Although the placement has been completed, RecruitOS continues to monitor the candidate throughout the agreed probation or replacement guarantee period.

This helps ensure that the agency remains aware of any issues before they become commercial disputes.

---

### **Step 4 — Monitoring Candidate Stability**

During probation, the recruiter continues maintaining periodic contact with the candidate.

RecruitOS also monitors any warning signals such as:

* Candidate considering resignation  
* Counter offers  
* Lack of communication  
* Early exit risks

If no risks are identified, the probation period continues normally.

---

### **Step 5 — Managing Early Exits**

If the candidate resigns or leaves the organization during the replacement guarantee period, RecruitOS immediately initiates the replacement workflow.

The system notifies the recruiter, Agency Owner, and other relevant stakeholders.

If the placement involved a recruitment partner, partner payouts remain on hold until the replacement process is completed.

RecruitOS also creates a replacement hiring mandate, allowing the recruiter to begin sourcing a replacement candidate without delay.

---

### **Step 6 — Completing Financial Settlement**

As client payments are received, RecruitOS helps the agency track invoice status.

If recruitment partners are involved, the Agency Owner reviews the placement and authorizes partner commission payments after all commercial obligations have been satisfied.

All financial approvals remain under management control.

---

### **Step 7 — Closing the Placement**

Once the probation period ends successfully:

* The placement is marked as successfully completed.  
* Financial records are finalized.  
* Partner settlements are completed.  
* The recruitment assignment is officially closed.

The candidate now becomes part of the agency's historical talent network and may be considered for future opportunities.

---

## **Key Business Decisions**

During this stage, several important business decisions are made.

The client decides:

* Whether the candidate has successfully joined.  
* Whether any replacement obligations need to be activated.

The recruiter decides:

* Whether the placement remains healthy.  
* Whether intervention is required during probation.

The Agency Owner decides:

* Whether financial settlements should proceed.  
* Whether replacement commitments have been fulfilled.  
* Whether partner commissions should be released.

---

## **What RecruitOS Does Automatically**

Throughout this workflow, RecruitOS continuously supports the agency by:

* Recording candidate joining.  
* Generating placement invoices.  
* Starting probation tracking.  
* Monitoring candidate stability.  
* Identifying high-risk placements.  
* Triggering replacement workflows when required.  
* Tracking invoice status.  
* Supporting partner settlement management.  
* Maintaining a complete history of every placement.

---

## **End of Chapter**

The recruitment lifecycle has now reached its conclusion.

The client has successfully hired a candidate.

The agency has completed its commercial obligations.

Revenue has been protected.

The placement has been formally closed.

RecruitOS preserves the complete history of the engagement, allowing the agency to learn from every placement and build a stronger talent network for future hiring assignments.
