## **1\. INDUSTRY BACKGROUND: HOW RECRUITMENT AGENCIES OPERATE & LOSE MONEY**

To pitch effectively, you must understand how a recruitment agency works and where their revenue breaks down every single day.

### **The Agency Business Model**

Recruitment agencies (also called staffing consultancies or search firms) act as external hiring partners for companies.

* **Commercial Terms:** Agencies charge a fee—typically **8.33% to 15% of the candidate's annual CTC** (roughly equivalent to 1 month of the candidate's salary) per successful hire.  
* **Payment Conditions:** The agency only gets paid *after* the candidate physically joins the client company and completes a 30 to 90-day probation period.  
* **The Sourcing Reality:** Recruiters manage open positions (mandates) by finding candidate resumes (CVs) on job boards like Naukri, Bayt, or LinkedIn, interviewing them, and presenting a shortlist to the client hiring manager.

### **The 4 Major Revenue Leaks in the Industry**

Agency owners are constantly stressed by four operational problems that directly destroy their profits:

| THE 4 MAJOR AGENCY REVENUE LEAKS |  |
| ----- | :---- |
| WhatsApp & Data Leakage | Recruiters talk to candidates on personal WhatsApp. When a recruiter quits, they take candidate data and relationships with them. |
| Slow Client Feedback  | Clients take 5–7 days to review emailed CVs.  In that time, top candidates take other jobs. |
| Notice-Period Drop-offs | Candidates serve 30–90 day notice periods and accept counter-offers without warning.  |
| Wasted Sourcing & CV Chaos | Past CVs sit in desktop folders. Recruiters pay for job boards to source the same profile twice instead of reusing past database leads. |

1. **WhatsApp & Data Leakage:** Over 80% of candidate communication happens on personal WhatsApp. Agency owners have zero visibility into what recruiters say, and if a recruiter resigns, years of candidate data and relationships walk out the door.  
2. **Client Review Friction:** Clients take days or weeks to review candidate shortlists sent via email attachments. By the time the hiring manager responds, the best candidates have already accepted another offer.  
3. **The 30 to 90-Day Notice Period Danger:** In regions like India and the Middle East, candidates must serve a 30, 60, or 90-day notice period before joining. Up to **20%–30% of placed candidates drop out or accept counter-offers** right before their start date, wiping out tens of thousands of dollars in anticipated agency revenue.  
4. **Wasted Sourcing & Duplicate Sourcing:** Recruiters save resumes in scattered desktop folders or spreadsheets. When a new job opens, they pay extra subscription money to job boards (Naukri/LinkedIn) to re-source candidates they already interviewed six months ago.

## **4\. IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP) & BUYER PERSONAS**

Focus outreach on decision-makers who feel the direct financial pain of lost hires.

### **Primary Target (Tier 1: High Conversion)**

* **Company Profile:** Boutique & Independent Recruitment Agencies, Executive Search Firms, and Staffing Consultancies.  
* **Team Size:** 5 to 50 Recruiters.  
* **Target Job Titles:** Founder, Managing Director (MD), Agency Owner, Managing Partner, Head of Operations.  
* **Geographic Scope:** India (Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru), UAE/GCC region (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh), and UK/US offshore sourcing agencies.

### **Why Target Agency Owners directly?**

Agency Owners pay for job boards, office overhead, and recruiter salaries out of their own pocket. When a client responds slowly or a candidate drops out on Day 45 of a notice period, **the Owner personally loses money**. They have the authority to make a purchase decision immediately without long corporate procurement processes.

### **Script 3: The 15-Minute Discovery Call Playbook (Questions to Ask)**

When an Agency Owner agrees to a call, do not start by listing product features. Instead, diagnose their current operational bottlenecks with these questions:

1. **Pipeline Volume:** *"How many active job mandates is your team currently managing across recruiters?"*  
2. **Client Feedback Delays:** *"When your recruiters send candidate shortlists via email, how long does it usually take client hiring managers to respond with feedback?"*  
3. **Data Loss & Communication:** *"How are your recruiters tracking candidate calls and WhatsApp messages? If a senior recruiter leaves tomorrow, do you retain their entire candidate chat history?"*  
4. **Notice-Period Drop-offs:** *"Out of every 10 candidates who accept an offer, how many drop out or accept counter-offers during their 30 to 90-day notice period?"*  
5. **The Pitch Transition:** *"If we could give your clients a 1-click review link on their phone and automatically alert you whenever a notice-period candidate goes dark, how much would that impact your monthly billing?"*
